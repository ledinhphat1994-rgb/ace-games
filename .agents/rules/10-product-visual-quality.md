# Digital Campus v2 — Product & Visual Quality Standards

1. **Target Aesthetic — Teen Digital Campus**:
   - Modern social app + teen digital campus + gaming polish + learning product.
   - NOT an enterprise admin dashboard, generic school portal, or AI-generated card wall.
2. **Reduce Before Adding**:
   - Strip unnecessary subtitles, decorative tags, redundant badges, and explanatory paragraphs.
   - Users should grasp the screen within ~3 seconds.
3. **Card Wall Reduction**:
   - Avoid boxing everything in heavy borders and rounded rectangles.
   - Let the layout breathe using open space, grouping, and subtle surface elevation.
4. **Color & Typography System**:
   - Deep neutral backgrounds with purposeful, controlled accents (primary, reward/achievement, success, warning, destructive).
   - High-contrast, clean typography scaled for display, section titles, and crisp metadata; avoid tiny illegible 10px text.
5. **Mobile as a First-Class Citizen**:
   - Mobile is not just shrunken desktop; prioritize critical touch points, use bottom sheets/drawers, and streamline headers.
